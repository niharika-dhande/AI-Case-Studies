# Agentic AI

A multi-agent runtime built from first principles and delivered to a client, deliberately without LangChain, LangGraph or CrewAI: an orchestrator routing to six specialist agents, with a confidence-gated router, handoff envelopes for state, sequential and parallel rounds, a conflict scan with revision rounds, per-run cost ceilings, resumable runs and human confirmation before external actions. Autonomous tool-using agent loops are the designed next phase, not a finished capability.

## Capabilities

- Custom multi-agent orchestration
- Confidence-gated intent routing
- Handoff-envelope state
- Conflict detection & revision
- Cost ceilings & resumable runs
- Human-in-the-loop
