# AI Automation & Integrations

Manual, repeatable work turned into pipelines: a design brief becomes print-ready assets and a production kit; an authenticated MCP gateway brings a third-party image model into Claude; a Google Workspace folder stays searchable through incremental ingestion; meetings become SDLC artifacts; booking changes drive WhatsApp notifications and reminders through a transactional outbox; and publishing in a CMS triggers site refresh and asset generation. Every event-driven step has a scheduled safety net, and AI drafts pass a human review gate.

## Capabilities

- Event-driven pipelines

- MCP connectors
- Incremental sync
- Human-in-the-loop review
- Confirm-before-act
